Strauss-RSPrefix your project's dependencies

A Rust port of Strauss for Composer-based projects. One binary, no prerequisites.

Getting started
Why prefix?
Configuration

  • Saves you time and money

    Up to 139x faster than the PHP version, drastically speeding up your CI/CD flow

  • Avoids clashes between dependencies

    Two projects (i.e. Wordpress plugins) can bundle different versions of the same library. Strauss-RS gives each plugin its own copy under its own namespace.

  • Reads your existing setup

    Works out defaults based on your composer.json file, with full customisation options.
